Stories with Sloffy Privacy Policy

Last updated: [[DATE THIS GOES LIVE]]

Stories with Sloffy makes personalised bedtime stories for children. A grown-up creates the account and sets up each child's explorer profile. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to get it deleted.

The short version. We collect the parent's email address, the details you give us about each child so their stories can feature them, and the stories themselves. We use AI services to write and illustrate stories. We show no advertising, we use no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Children never type information into the app: a parent enters everything about them.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, attribution, or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or device address book.
  • No audio recordings. Stories With Sloffy plays narration; it never listens.
  • No payment card details. Card data goes to Apple or Google and never reaches us.

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our instructions, only to provide the service, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes — including training their own AI models. Sharing with them is part of how the app works: without them a story cannot be written, illustrated or read aloud.

Children's privacy

Stories with Sloffy is made for children under 13, and we follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It is designed for a grown-up to set up and a child to enjoy.

  • Parents enter everything. A parent creates the account and enters each child's details. Every place in the app where information can be typed is either part of the parent's sign-up or sits behind a grown-up check, as do purchases, account settings and links that leave the app. Children are never asked to provide information themselves.
  • What is collected while a child uses the app is limited to the technical information described above, used only to keep the app working.
  • How a child's details are used: solely to write, illustrate and read aloud that child's stories, and to show them again in the library. They are shared only with the service providers listed above, for that purpose.
  • No chat, social features, user-to-user contact, public posting or advertising anywhere in the app.

Your rights as a parent

  • Review your child's details in the app (Account → Explorer profiles), and their stories in the library. Email us for a copy of everything we hold.
  • Change any detail in the app at any time.
  • Delete a child's details and stories (Account → Explorer profiles → Remove), or the whole account (Account → Delete account).
  • Refuse further collection or use at any time by removing the child's profile or deleting the account. We stop collecting and using that child's information immediately.

If you contact us by email about your child's information, we will reply to the email address on the account, or otherwise confirm you are the account holder, before sharing or deleting anything.

Storage and security

All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted per account, so one account cannot read another's children or stories. Story illustrations are held in private storage, filed under the account that created them and readable by no one else — not by another account, and not by anyone with the address. The app opens them through short-lived signed links that stop working after a week. Narration is also held in private storage and is only ever played back through the signed-in account that created it. We keep a written information security program describing these safeguards and review it at least once a year.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it to provide the app. The one exception is the narration history kept by our voice provider, ElevenLabs, described at the end of this list.

  • Account, child profiles and saved stories: until you delete them, or until the account has not been used for 12 months, in which case we delete it in full after emailing you 30 days beforehand. Opening the app counts as using it. Accounts with an active subscription are never treated as inactive.
  • Illustrations no saved story uses, for example from a story you did not save or have deleted: removed within 30 days.
  • Narration audio: removed within 90 days of being made, whether or not the story is kept. It is made again if needed.
  • Narration on your device: the app keeps a copy of narration it has played in its cache, until the app or your device clears it.
  • Daily request counts: removed after 7 days.
  • Deleting a child's profile deletes their details and saved stories immediately. Deleting your account deletes the account, every child profile, every saved story, and all its illustrations and narration immediately.
  • Backups: we do not currently keep backups of our database. If we add them, they will expire within 7 days, so deleted information will be gone from them within that time.
  • Our service providers: Anthropic deletes story requests within 30 days, and OpenAI keeps image requests in its abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Either may keep a request longer if it is flagged for misuse or the law requires it. fal.ai does not store the requests, and deletes its copy of each illustration within an hour.
  • ElevenLabs keeps the narration it makes, and the page text it was made from (which includes your child's name), in our account's history until it is deleted. We do not currently delete it automatically, so deleting a profile or your account does not remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your data: profile details are editable in the app; email us for anything you cannot reach.
  • Delete a child's data: Account → Explorer profiles → Remove.
  • Delete your account and everything in it: Account → Delete account. It takes effect immediately. You can also email support@storieswithsloffy.com and we will action it within 30 days.
  • Get a copy of your data: email us and we will send an export.
  • Manage your subscription: in your App Store or Play Store account settings. Billing and cancellation are handled by the store, not by us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or state privacy laws such as the CCPA — including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, our lawful basis is the contract with you for running the service, and consent for the child details you choose to provide.

International transfers

Our database is in the United States, and our service providers process data mainly in the United States. If you use the app from outside the US, your information is transferred there; where the law requires it, we rely on our providers' standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If we change what we collect about children, how we use it, or who we share it with, we will tell you in the app and by email before the change takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
